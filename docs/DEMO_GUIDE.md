# Meeting demo guide

## Five-minute flagship journey

1. Open the public link and point out the permanent **Unofficial concept** label.
2. On Home, show Alex's usual Thursday plan: Harbour Family Hotel, Table 23, six people at 6:45 pm and the 8:15 pm member draw.
3. Select **Confirm visit**, then open **Visit** and **Choose table**.
4. Select Table 23 on the original customer-safe floor plan. Call out the quieter family location, step-free path and Screen 7.
5. Open **Ride**, confirm two inbound passengers and six return passengers, then show why only a broad window appears before approach.
6. Return to **Visit** and select **Simulate Table 23 check-in**. Home is now in Tonight mode.
7. Open **Order**, choose the chicken parmigiana and **Use my usual**. Confirm the barbecue base, chips and salad, no dressing, and the $26.90 demo price. Confirm the simulated order and show the Table 23 group.
8. In **Visit**, request the Cowboys game. Explain that Screen 7 can change while Sports Bar racing screens remain locked.
9. Open **Rewards** to show fabricated points, 17 entries, the fictional draw and the non-functional demo card.
10. Finish in **Me**. Edit or pause a memory, then focus on **Never used for recommendations**.

## Recovery

- **Reset demo** restores the flagship state and local persistence.
- State selectors can repair booking, ride, order, service and kitchen conditions without a reload.
- Press Escape to close Ask Star or Demo Controls.

Everything seen in this guide is fabricated and local to the browser.
